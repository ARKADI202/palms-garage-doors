HOW TO READ THIS new here? plain-English guide

This is a poster that watches a real money market and updates its own numbers every few seconds — you don't type anything (except the two hand-entered fields noted at the bottom).

1 · Live Mark Price

The price of one unit, right now. It changes by itself as people buy and sell. Green = going up, red = going down. It also shows yesterday's price so you can compare.

2 · Implied Valuation

If one unit costs $X and there are N of them, this is what all of them together are worth — price × count — shown as one big number.

3 · Live Trade Tape

A live scoreboard of every buy and sell. Green line = someone bought, red line = someone sold. It also counts how busy the last 60 seconds were.

4 · Saylor vs BTC ETF Flows

Compares how much Bitcoin Michael Saylor's company scooped up vs how much money flowed into Bitcoin ETFs. The big "×" is how many times bigger his pile is; its worth updates live with the price.

Where the numbers come from: a live connection to the Hyperliquid exchange (price & trades) and CoinGecko (holdings). Price and trades are truly instant. Saylor's amount only changes when he announces it (about weekly), so the two dollar figures in panel 4 are typed in by hand and you update them when new news drops.
